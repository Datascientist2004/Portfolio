// ABOUT ME

Data & AI solutions, delivered.

From teaching Python to 100+ students, to corporate BI reporting, to shipping a live AI product — here's the full story.

I'm Honey Chauhan — an independent Data & AI Solutions Consultant specializing in Business Intelligence, Predictive Analytics, and Web Automation. I help growing businesses convert scattered, messy data into clean, automated dashboards and decision tools that actually get used.

I started by teaching 100+ students Python and SQL, then moved into corporate KPI reporting at Startek, then built Power BI dashboards and ETL pipelines at Cognifyz Technologies. I've since built real projects — a diabetes detection model, a music recommendation system, RFM customer segmentation, an AI chatbot, a Netflix Power BI dashboard — all live on GitHub — and FitFuel AI Guru, a full-stack AI fitness & nutrition coaching app I designed, built and shipped end-to-end, live in production today.

IBM-certified across Data Analysis, Business Analysis, and Generative AI. Based in Delhi NCR, working with clients globally — open for freelance projects and monthly retainers.
